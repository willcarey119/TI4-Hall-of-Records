How to read this

Each section below is one element of the product — not a page. We give 3 to 6 variants, each with a "When to use" and a "Watch out for" annotation. Drag the canvas to pan, scroll to zoom, click any artboard to open it fullscreen.

Variants are intentionally interoperable: the chip from §02 is the same chip used in §05's match cards. Pick a variant per element and the composed pages stay coherent.

The current Hall of Records is solid in structure — Home / Games / League / Agenda — but every surface mixes too many type sizes and hierarchy is fuzzy. This kit picks one editorial spine (newspaper broadsheet) and rationalises every primitive against it.

Type system

Newsreader (display, body), IBM Plex Sans (UI), IBM Plex Mono (labels + tabular numerics). One scale per role. No more competing headings.

Color

Warm-paper neutrals (oklch 0.97–0.18, chroma ≤0.016). One accent — vermillion (oklch 0.45 / 0.12 / 25) — reserved for "the answer". Faction color stays in the data layer; the chrome stays editorial.

Composition

Every card is a kicker · headline · deck · body · rule unit. Newspaper bones. The hierarchy is the design — not the borders, not the shadows. Cards have neither.
